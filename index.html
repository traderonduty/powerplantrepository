WESM / grid data tool

Power plant master repository

Drop in CAPEG and CAPER CSV files. This builds one master table — alias, resource code, capacity, region, technology, current outage status, and reserve product type with its max capacity — sorted and ready to download as Excel.

1. Add files

Any number of CAPEG and CAPER files, in .csv or .xlsx. Type is auto-detected from each file's columns, not its name or extension — and a multi-tab .xlsx gets every sheet checked individually.

Drag CSV or Excel files here, or click to browse

CAPEG = generation capacity · CAPER = reserve/ancillary capacity · a Resource_ID + Technology CSV refreshes the technology lookup · a Resource_ID + Status CSV refreshes the outage log · a Short_Name + Participant_Name CSV refreshes the WESM participants registry

Upload a master repository .xlsx you downloaded from this tool before, and changes since then get flagged automatically — new outages, restored plants, capacity or technology changes, new or missing units.
